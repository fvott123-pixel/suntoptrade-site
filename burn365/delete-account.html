You can have your Burn365 account and everything in it permanently deleted.

How to request it

In the app: sign in, then go to Profile (More) → Delete account and type DELETE. Your account and everything in it is deleted straight away.

Can't sign in? Email sales@suntoptrade.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Burn365 account”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription — cancel that in your phone's subscription settings.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email address and password.
  • Your profile, goals and plan.
  • Every meal, weight, water, workout and health-sync record.
  • Your progress photos.
  • Your entire conversation history with Maya.

What may be kept

Only records we are legally required to retain, such as records of subscription purchases. These are kept for the minimum period the law requires and are never used for anything else.
