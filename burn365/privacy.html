Burn365 is operated by Sun Top Trade Co., Limited. This policy explains what the Burn365 app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, display name and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Health and wellbeing data: weight, height, age, goals, meals, calories and macros, water, workouts, steps, progress photos, and how you say you're feeling.
  • Conversations with Maya: what you type or say to her, and her replies.
  • Preferences: reminder times, timezone, coaching style.

Why we collect it

Solely to run the service for you: to build your plan, track what you log, let Maya remember you between conversations, and send the check-ins you asked for. We do not sell your data. We do not show adverts. We do not use your conversations to train anyone's AI models.

Who it is shared with

  • OpenAI — what you write to Maya, and any meal or progress photo you ask her to read, is sent to OpenAI to generate her replies and estimates. If you use an optional voice feature, what you say is also sent to OpenAI to understand it. This is necessary for Maya to work at all.
  • Our hosting and database providers — to store your account and your logs.
  • Nobody else. We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or insurers. We will disclose data if the law compels us to.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you connect a watch or phone health source, we read steps, active calories, exercise minutes, resting heart rate and sleep, only after you grant permission. It is used to show your movement in the app and is never shared or sold. You can revoke access at any time in your phone's settings, and in More → Movement & tracking.

Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form. The server identifies you from your signed-in session — it never trusts an account id sent from a phone, so one person's data cannot be read by editing a request.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or delete your account and everything in it. Email sales@suntoptrade.com and we will action it within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handled it you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong, or to the privacy regulator in the country you live in.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your profile, logs, photos and conversations from our database.

Delete your account

In the app: sign in, then go to Profile (More) → Delete account and type DELETE. Your account and everything in it is deleted straight away.

Can't sign in? Email sales@suntoptrade.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Burn365 account”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.
