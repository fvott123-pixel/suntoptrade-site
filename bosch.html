Australia compliance & supply notes

  • Power: All Bosch locks ship with 4× AA alkaline battery operation (no AC required). USB-C emergency boost port on FU/T series. No 240V wiring needed — passes AU electrical regs without electrician sign-off.
  • Door spec: Locks fit standard AU door thicknesses (35–60mm) with included mounting kit. We confirm your door spec before factory dispatch.
  • App & cloud: Bosch's Tuya-compatible app works in Australia. Optional Wi-Fi hub adds remote unlock + audit log streaming. We can pre-pair before shipment for boutique-hotel deployments.
  • Warranty: 12 months standard, 24 months available — replacements confirmed by fault video and coordinated through Sun Top's Australian stock, typically within 2 business days.
  • MOQ: Mixed-model orders welcome from 30 units up. Sea freight DDP door-to-door from approximately 5–7 weeks; air freight available at quote.
